Add Halo CE Mobile to your Home Screen

  1. Tap Share in Safari's toolbar (at the bottom on iPhone, at the top on iPad).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open HCEMobile from your Home Screen. It runs full screen and keeps your data.

In Chrome or Edge on iPhone, Share is at the top right. iOS only lets you add a site to the Home Screen yourself, so you only need to do this once.

Install the app

  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.
  3. Open HCEMobile from your home screen or app drawer.

In Samsung Internet, tap ☰, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Game data

To play, choose your own Xbox disc image of Halo: Combat Evolved (.iso or .xiso, a verified copy). The game's maps folder is copied into the app's private storage (up to 1.8 GB), after which you can delete the disc image. Nothing is uploaded.

A direct download link to your own disc image, for example from Dropbox, Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 or your own web server. Only the game's maps folder (about 1.8 GB) is downloaded. Google Drive and other sharing pages don't work.

Only verified copies of Halo: Combat Evolved for the original Xbox can be added. You can add more than one and choose which to play; each keeps its own saved games.

Play online

Sign in or create a free account to play online. The campaign, split screen and System Link don't need one.

Your account

One free account works on every device you play on. It gives you:

  • A unique gamertag that other players see
  • Matchmaking with ranks from 1 to 50 in each ranked playlist
  • Custom Games lobbies you can host, join and invite friends to
  • A friends list, private messages and clans
  • Lobby and global chat

Signed in as

You can change the capitalization of your gamertag (bob, Bob, BOB), but not the gamertag itself.

Co-op

The host runs the game and streams player 2's view to their friend, who doesn't need the game data. The host chooses Multiplayer > Cooperative Play for the campaign (full game only) or Split Screen, and the friend presses Jump (A) to join as player 2.

Lobbies

Features

The complete game

The full campaign and multiplayer of Halo: Combat Evolved, running natively from your own disc image.

Matchmaking

Social, Ranked and Experimental playlists, Halo 2-style ranks from 1 to 50, and party searching with your friends.

Custom Games

Host or browse public lobbies with full game settings: map, game type, score, weapons, shields and more. Join games in progress.

Friends and clans

See where your friends are and join them in one tap. Private messages, invites, and clans with Halo 2 ranks.

Chat

Lobby chat, global chat and private messages, available in the game and on this page.

Your controls

Two touch layouts, including an Original Xbox controller, fully editable. Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, MFi and Backbone controllers, or keyboard and mouse.

Co-op and local play

Split screen for up to four players, System Link, and remote co-op that streams player 2's view to a friend's device.

Play anywhere

Install it on iPhone, iPad, Android or your computer. Plays full screen and offline, at up to your screen's native resolution.

Saves and updates

Keep several copies of the game, each with its own saved games. Export and restore them anytime; updates install automatically.

Keyboard controls

Click an action, then press the key or mouse button you want for it (for Left click, click the waiting button again). A key already in use swaps with it; Esc cancels. Enter, Backspace and the arrow keys stay as the menus' keys.

If models or the menu's ship look stretched or broken, try A, B and C (each takes effect on the next launch) and tell us which looks right.

While the game is running, the pause menu's "Report a graphics problem" sends us a picture of what it's showing, with what your phone's graphics chip gets wrong.

Custom TURN relay (optional): helps online play on networks that block direct connections, such as some mobile carriers.
